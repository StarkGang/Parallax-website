# Business rules

## Pass and price

Every participant must obtain an independent ₹350 convention pass (seeded default; previously ₹500 in earlier planning drafts). The pass includes lunch on both days. It is not an event wallet or a discount balance. The pass price does not enter the included-event base-value sum.

The demo allowance is up to 3 eligible events, with an illustrative ₹450 cap. The sum of selected `baseValue` must be at most the cap. ₹450 is provisional; the current planning discussion mentions ₹400 or ₹450. The final count and whether it is exactly or up to the count are pending approval. Additional events use their `addonPrice` separately and require the convention pass first. Base value and add-on price are independent configured values.

Each team participant must have their own valid pass before a team registration is confirmed unless the edition administrator configures a different approved policy. Student eligibility ultimately requires actual verification; the prototype makes no such claim. Payment and confirmation email are not implemented; email is optional for a future application.

## Demo behavior

Selections are in page state only. No payment is attempted and no successful state is fabricated. Event dates, venue rooms, event rules, capacity, prices, statuses and participant rows are illustrative. Refund/cancellation terms are not yet approved.
